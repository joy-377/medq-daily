About MedQ Daily

Last updated: 15 November 2026

MedQ Daily is a student-focused educational platform designed to support medical students with daily MCQs, subject-wise question practice, mock tests, clinical learning resources and progress tracking.

Our mission

To help medical students connect theoretical knowledge with clinical application through accessible, high-yield practice.

What the platform offers

  • Daily MCQ practice and explanations.
  • Subject-wise question banks and custom tests.
  • NEET PG and INI-CET-oriented practice.
  • Test history, bookmarks and mistake review.
  • Clinical learning articles and image-based flashcards.

MedQ Daily is an independent educational resource and is not affiliated with any examination authority.
