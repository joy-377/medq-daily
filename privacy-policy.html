Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

MedQ Daily respects your privacy. This policy explains the information that may be handled when you use this educational website.

Information we collect

If you choose to sign in with Google, we may receive your name, email address and profile picture for account identification and progress synchronisation. You can use the local study features in Guest Mode. Study activity such as test scores, solved questions, bookmarks and streaks may be stored in your browser and, when signed in, synchronised to Firebase.

How information is used

  • To provide and maintain the MCQ, quiz and test features.
  • To save preferences, bookmarks and study progress.
  • To improve the reliability and educational experience of the website.
  • To respond to support requests.

Cookies, local storage and advertising

MedQ Daily uses browser local storage and similar technologies to remember preferences and progress. Google AdSense and other third-party vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure advertisements. You can manage cookie settings through your browser and Google ad settings.

Third-party services

The website may use Google Firebase for authentication and cloud data synchronisation, and Google AdSense for advertising. These providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Local data can be cleared through your browser settings; cloud data associated with a signed-in account may require a support request.

Children and sensitive information

MedQ Daily is an educational resource for medical students. Please do not submit sensitive personal or patient-identifying information.
